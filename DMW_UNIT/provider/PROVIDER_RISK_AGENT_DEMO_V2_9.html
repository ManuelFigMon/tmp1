About DMW Data Mart Workbench

The DMW – Data Mart Workbench v1.0Beta (Cortex Provider Analyst) is powered by Snowflake Cortex Analyst and the FFS_PROVIDER_DEMO semantic view over FFS_PROVIDER_DEMO_FLAT.

Ask plain-English questions about ~279 K Medicare DME providers. The app converts your question to SQL, executes it, and returns a colour-coded results table.

Risk tiers:  HIGH score ≥ 40  |  MEDIUM 25–39  |  LOW < 25

The DMW is modeled on the CMS Episode Workbench. Long descriptions are AI-generated and should be independently verified.

Help & Support

Type a question about DME provider fraud risk in the input box and press Send, or click a sample question in the sidebar.

Charts: just ask for one in the chat — “risk tier distribution as a pie chart”, “bar chart of average score by FAAIR type”, “line chart of…”, “heatmap of provider counts by state and risk tier”. To re-draw the last result, say “show this as a bar chart”.

Key columns:

  • FRAUD_RISK_SCORE — base risk score 0–100
  • MULTI_STATE_RISK_SCORE — enhanced score including state footprint
  • RISK_TIER — HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW
  • PRVDR_DME_FAAIR_TYPE_CD — A=Standard, R=Revoked, S=Suspended, T=Terminated
  • STATE_COUNT — states spanned in ownership records
